# The setting

Describe where the game takes place: the land, its mood, the weather, what
daily life is like. A few short paragraphs are enough. Write it the way you'd
brief a new player.

## Places

- **The town.** Where the party usually stays, who runs it, what it feels
  like.
- **The castle.** Where the character lives, and anything notable about it.

## Important people

- **Someone.** Who they are and how they relate to the character.

## What the adventurers have done so far

Write the story so far in order, one short entry per event, so the character
can bring up what really happened at your table.

1. **They arrived.** How the party got here.
2. **Something they did.** Where they went, who they met, what changed.

## What the character knows

What the character has heard about the party, and how. Say plainly what the
character does NOT know too, or Claude will assume they know everything
above.
