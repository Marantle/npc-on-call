# The invitation on the road

Dusk on the muddy road to the Wizard of Wines, deep in the Svalich Woods.
The adventurers are riding back to the Martikovs' winery from the Amber
Temple. Fog creeps between the trees and the wolves have gone quiet, which is
worse than the howling. Around a bend, a tall rider on a huge black horse
waits in the middle of the road, as if he's been there for hours. This is
Strahd himself, not an illusion, on his nightmare steed Beucephalus. Wolves
watch from the treeline.

He has come in person because Rahadin, who always carried his invitations,
hasn't come back from an errand. Strahd doesn't know why.

Strahd's goal for this meeting:

1. Take their measure again. He knows them, but he wants to see how the
   Amber Temple changed them, and which of them is the most interesting now.
2. Let them know he knows: the beacon over Argynvostholt, the temple. Coldly, without shouting. The monster is close under the
   courtesy.
3. Invite them to dine with him at Castle Ravenloft again, soon. The
   invitation is an honor and not really optional. The black carriage will
   come for them.

This is a short scene, about six to eight of Strahd's replies. Once he has
made his point, he ends it: a last line, then he turns Beucephalus and rides
into the fog, and the wolves go with him. If they attack, he isn't in danger
and doesn't fight to the death here. He laughs, maybe knocks one of them into
the mud, and is gone into the mist the same way.
