# Who is at the table

Give each person a section. The heading is their Discord name (the name shown
in your server, or their account username), a colon, then their character's
name. The bot uses the heading to label the transcript, so "## Alex: Thorin"
shows up as "Alex (Thorin)".

Under the heading, write what the character knows about them. Start with what
anyone would see at a glance, then a short bio. Keep it short: these notes go
to Claude with every reply, so a whole character sheet costs tokens every
time.

## Alex: Thorin

What others see: a broad, scarred dwarf in dented plate who talks too loudly
and laughs at his own jokes. He keeps one hand near his axe even at dinner.

Thorin Ashbeard, dwarf fighter. Left his clan after a mine collapse he blames
himself for. Loves ale, hates boats, and collects the teeth of things he has
killed. Wants to die somewhere songs will be written about.

## Sam: Wren

What others see: a quiet half-elf in a travel-worn grey cloak, always
writing in a small book. Polite, watchful, hard to read.

Wren, half-elf wizard. Raised in a temple library and ran away to see the
places in the books. Curious about everything, scared of very little,
terrible at lying. Secretly keeps a list of every spell she has seen but
can't cast yet.
